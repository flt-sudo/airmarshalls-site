Indoor Air Quality, Asbestos, LEED, Mold and Moisture Sampling / Inspections / Assessments

Highly specialized Indoor Air Quality, Mold and moisture sampling/inspections are recommended when more significant complex contaminant situations are believed to be present in a structure. These specialized sampling/inspections are performed in cases where health concerns are believed to be directly related to poor Indoor Air quality. Also, highly specialized Mold and moisture inspections are strongly recommended during real estate transactions, and legal concerns between the buyer/seller, tenant/landlord, or insured property owner and the insurance company.

Thermal Imaging

Infrared Thermal Imaging affords us the ability to discover hidden moisture areas and areas of energy loss. Thermal imaging is a major systematic fact finding instrument in a structure investigation. Although impossible to see with the naked eye, these hidden conditions, if not detected, could cause serious structural damage, and Mold growth that could cause Mold related health issues.
